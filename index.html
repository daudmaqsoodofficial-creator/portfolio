02 · Current role · Sharooq Al Jubail · 2025 to date

No handover. No records. Control established.

Joined as Facility Operations Coordinator in October 2025. Promoted to Assistant Manager, Facility and Business Operations in September 2026, eleven months later.

Position

On appointment there was no handover, no documentation and no checklists. Maintenance and diesel spend held no traceable record across a mixed fleet of more than 150 units.

Measures taken

The operating record was built from zero. Workshop supervisors, site foremen, coordinators and technicians were given registers, forms and procedures that fit how they work, then held to them on every service, refill and bill.

Outcome

Cost leakage of SAR 40,000 to 50,000 a month was curtailed. Leadership now reads the position from a dashboard. The remit was widened to the commercial desk, vendors and marketing.

02Handover forms, checklists and gate passes issued to site and workshop
03Preventive maintenance set on a date or kilometre trigger
04Diesel and engine oil tested against work output, by project
05Bills audited against cash sent; variances traced and recovered
